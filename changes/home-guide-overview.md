- Home now opens from the menu, with Continue Guide or Choose a Guide,
  your open guides and contextual upcoming-guide recommendations.
  Guides keeps Browse, Current and Recent; explicit guide links keep their
  destinations. Home preserves the existing branding and supports every skin.
  A contextual Your Journey header and seven reusable cards organize leveling,
  talents, dungeons, upcoming guides, training, gear sources and nearby rares.
  The details region can be dismissed to widen the workspace; narrower windows
  reflow and fit the dashboard to the window without a scrollbar. Open guides remain available below; the Suggested for you section is removed.
  Select a card or open guide to inspect it without leaving Home. Guide details
  show available quests, route facts and authored rewards; talent plans show
  their picks and notes. Inspector tabs and scroll positions are remembered for
  the session, with explicit actions kept separate from selection.
  Continue now shows live step context and authoritative guide progress for the
  active guide's category. Leveling Build shows the selected plan, next planned
  pick and talent-state warnings. Dungeon matches share the journal's level and
  faction filters, with known quests, rewards and explicit map/entrance actions
  in the inspector. Up Next distinguishes the Engine's continuation from optional
  suggestions. Hidden Home defers provider refreshes until it is shown again.
  Class Training now shows reminder context and the currently open class
  trainer's listed services, with an explicit trainer action. Gear Opportunities
  compares contextual quest rewards and equipped items using existing Gear
  weights, keeping available rewards separate from potential upgrades. Rares
  Nearby shows recent detections or past sightings in your zone, with known
  spawns and sighting locations labelled separately in its inspector.
  Customize Home now saves account-wide card visibility and order, with
  move-up/down controls and Restore defaults; Continue/Choose a Guide stays
  first. Details open on selection, supporting cards share aligned actions,
  and short windows move the inspector into the workspace scroll. Keyboard
  and gamepad focus reveal nested controls. Hidden cards and dismissed details
  defer their data work, and skin changes reuse the current snapshot.
